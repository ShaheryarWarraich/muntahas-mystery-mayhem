# Muntaha's Mystery Mayhem

A 2.5D detective mystery game designed by **Muntaha (age 9)** for herself and her little sister **Anya**. Make your own detective, sit in your agency office until a client knocks, then travel to the scene of the mystery — drag the room around, hunt for hidden clues, question the suspects, catch them in a lie, and name the thief.

**Play it here:** https://shaheryarwarraich.github.io/muntahas-mystery-mayhem/

## What's in it

- **Character creator** — 6 skin tones, 8 hairstyles (ponytail, space buns, braids, curly…), 8 hair colours, 6 outfits, accessories. Everyone is drawn facing forward, and there's a 🎲 Surprise Me button.
- **Set in DHA Lahore** — a bird's-eye city map with the DHA Office, Rafiq Tailors, Khan Sweets, chai khokhas, kite-filled skies, and aunties in shalwar kameez. Clues become leads, leads light up buildings, witnesses clear the innocent, and the Mystery Board pins Motive + Means + Opportunity before the Poirot-style gathering.
- **Ten playable cases** — from *The Stolen Recipe Book* (Auntie Parveen's bakery) to *The Hamster Escape* (Captain Nibbles, three-time Pet Parade champion). Rival bakers, jealous gardeners, sneaky nephews — real culprits with real motives, all gently reformed by the end.
- **2.5D scenes** — rooms are wider than the screen with parallax depth; drag to look around, tap cushions, jars, drawers and rugs to search underneath.
- **Press & Present interrogation** — press suspects for more detail, present a clue that contradicts their story, and watch their alibi fall apart. You can't accuse until you've cracked the culprit's final lie.
- **Evidence-backed accusation** — you pick *who* **and** *the clue that proves it*. Wrong guesses are gentle: a giggle and a retry.
- **Coins, gear and a squad** — 20 coins a case plus bonuses, six pieces of detective gear (the magnifying glass gives a real zoom lens), and seven unlockable detectives including a cat.
- 30 cases planned; cases 1–10 are playable now. A golden pawprint is hidden somewhere in case 8…

## Notes

- One single HTML file, no build tools, no dependencies — canvas 2D and WebAudio only.
- Works with mouse or touch; designed portrait for a tablet or phone.
- Progress saves in your browser, per player name. Add `?player=Muntaha` to the link to skip the name screen.
